# Changelog

## 0.3.2 — 2026-11-11

- Show the current temperature in Celsius, smaller and lighter, to the right of the Fahrenheit reading.

## 0.3.1 — 2026-11-11

- Right-align the version number on the display.

## 0.3.0 — 2026-11-11

- Show the installed add-on version on the display.

## 0.2.0 — 2026-11-10

- Initial release: renders a National Weather Service forecast as a 1072×1448, 16-level grayscale PNG for a jailbroken Kindle Paperwhite 3, plus a `/sleep` endpoint that tells the Kindle how long to deep-sleep.
- Optional Home Assistant sensor for the headline temperature, with fallback to the NWS observation.
- 12-hour precipitation chart, or a temperature curve when dry.
- 5-day forecast, sunrise/sunset and Kindle battery level.
